# Problem Statement

## Target Users

The main target users of RescueSlot are food rescue organisations, restaurants or food providers, and volunteers involved in food collection activities.

Food providers such as restaurants, bakeries and cafes can use the system to improve coordination when donating surplus food. Food rescue organisations can reduce manual scheduling effort by automatically matching available volunteers with suitable food pickup tasks. Volunteers can receive clearer pickup assignments based on their availability and vehicle capability.

---

## Evidence

Food loss and waste is a significant global challenge that affects food security, sustainability and the efficiency of food systems. The Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) highlighted that reducing food loss and waste is important for improving food security, increasing resource efficiency and supporting sustainable food systems (Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, 2019).

Although surplus food can provide support to communities in need, effective food rescue requires proper coordination to ensure that food is collected and redistributed before it becomes unsuitable for consumption. In practice, food rescue activities involve multiple operational factors, including food availability, volunteer schedules, vehicle capacity and collection deadlines.

Therefore, an intelligent scheduling approach is required to support better coordination between food providers, rescue organisations and volunteers while reducing the possibility of failed or delayed food pickups.

---

## Urgency and Social Value

Food rescue activities require timely collection because surplus food may lose its suitability for redistribution if it is not collected within an appropriate timeframe. Poor coordination between food providers and volunteers may result in delayed pickups, inefficient resource utilisation and avoidable food waste.

RescueSlot addresses this challenge by applying Artificial Intelligence techniques to automatically generate suitable volunteer assignments based on predefined constraints. The system applies Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP) with Backtracking Search and Minimum Remaining Values (MRV) heuristic to evaluate possible volunteer assignments while considering important factors such as volunteer availability, vehicle capacity and pickup requirements.

By improving scheduling efficiency between food providers, rescue organisations and volunteers, RescueSlot can contribute towards reducing avoidable food waste and supporting more sustainable food redistribution practices.

---

## SDG Alignment

RescueSlot aligns with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.

The project supports SDG 12 by promoting better utilisation of available food resources and reducing unnecessary waste through improved food rescue coordination. According to United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 12, responsible consumption and production focus on improving resource efficiency and reducing waste generation (United Nations, n.d.).

By helping ensure that suitable volunteers are assigned to collect surplus food before expiry, RescueSlot demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can support sustainable resource management and encourage responsible consumption practices.
